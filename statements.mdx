## Statement regarding our Partnership program's termination (31/07/2025)

Today, we’re releasing this statement to **everyone within our communities,** including our partners, to address recent decisions concerning the ilike’s Productions **Partnership program**.

After thorough analysis, we've made the choice to **terminate this Partnership program until further notice, which is effective immediately**. This choice follows a comprehensive review prompted by multiple challenges which impacts the program’s alignment with our values of **Innovation, Ambition and Authenticity**.

Firstly, **ongoing community-related issues** have created an environment that is unhelpful to upholding professional relationships. In particular, recent disputes involving **RoCanvas Productions** and its **associates**, including **its owner (Ren)**, have significantly undermined the collaborative spirit which our Partnership Program was founded upon.

We also wish to d**irectly address several ongoing concerns regarding RoCanvas Productions**, such as:

- **Harassment** directed at members of the ilike’s Productions team and our community.
- **Attempts to discredit ilike Productions** through misinformation and targeted actions.
- **False allegations of blackmailing** made against our founder and representatives of ilike’s Productions, which are **entirely false**.

Such behavior is **unacceptable**. In conclusion, once our partnership program fully returns, we will begin implementing **stricter procedures**, including the **blacklisting of RoCanvas Productions, its owner (Ren), and any directly affiliated entities from participating in the program**. This action is necessary to protect our community from further disruption and to uphold the standards of conduct expected of our partners.

Secondly, **a period of prolonged inactivity** has limited our ability to maintain meaningful engagement with partners, and has resulted in **reduced community growth**. Partnerships require **active collaboration**, and we recognise that these essential components have **not been sustained at the level we accept**.

Due to these reasons, **from now,** **our Partnership program will remain closed** as we take the time to **completely restructure how it will run going forward**. This process will involve **reassessing application procedures**, **redefining the standards that are expected, and creating clearer policies** to ensure that future partnerships are consistent with our values.

For the **current partners who wish to remain in the program**, we ask that you **directly contact our founder to discuss your involvement**. These requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

We understand that this is disappointing news for communities that considered partnering with us. However, we firmly believe that this temporary termination is a step towards building a **more sustainable and value-driven program**.

We appreciate your understanding and continued support. We'll be posting any further updates regarding the future of the program publicly via our Discord community.
