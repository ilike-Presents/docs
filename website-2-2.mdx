## Update v2026.1 — 02/01/2026 @ 7:00pm UTC.

<Danger>
  We apologise for the delay of approximately 3 weeks in publishing this changelog; this changelog was originally scheduled to be released on the 1st of January 2026 but it was published on the 31st of January 2026 instead.
</Danger>

We are pleased to **announce a website refresh for ilike Presents**, with **updates to navigation, visual consistency and overall structure across the site!**

#### What has changed?

- The **Contact** footer link has been updated to direct users to the official ilike Presents Discord server, replacing our Fourthwall contact page.

![](https://i.postimg.cc/TYrjbvgg/image.png)

- The **Status** footer link now links directly to our live service status page, rather than an internal ilike Presents page containing an embedded status view.

![](https://i.postimg.cc/hGGVNJw8/image.png)

- The ilike Presents website is now **powered by Elfsight widgets\***, allowing us to more easily add and manage new features. Additional widgets will be introduced in future updates.
- Our **Instagram** has been removed from the home page as we have made the decision to fully shutdown the account on the evening of the 3rd of February 2026.
- The **Guilded community link** has been removed from the Communities page, following the closure of the Guilded platform.

![](https://i.postimg.cc/Qt91x2j7/image.png)

#### Design Changes

- The positioning of content across the site has been updated to improve layout and navigation.
- The site’s theming has been refreshed to provide a more consistent and cohesive look across all pages.

#### Blog Website Updates

- **Changelog pages have received updated branding**, aligning them more closely with the refreshed ilike Presents website.
- A **new cookie consent prompt** has been introduced to provide clearer user choice and improved compliance.

![](https://i.postimg.cc/1zgfpDbN/image.png)

- A new **“Export as PDF” option** has been added to changelog pages, allowing updates to be downloaded and archived more easily.

![](https://i.postimg.cc/brRJzgSG/image.png)

#### Additional notes

- Please note that **one or two planned changes could not be completed** during this period **due to technical issues outside of our control**. We **apologise for any inconvenience this may cause** and can confirm that these changes **will be implemented in future updates**.

This refresh **does not introduce major functional changes**, but instead **focuses on improving structure, visual consistency, and long-term maintainability** across the website.

Thank you for your continued support.
