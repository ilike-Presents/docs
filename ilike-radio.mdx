## The latest ilikeRadio updates — 06/02/2026 @ 8:00pm UTC

We are pleased to **announce the most recent updates for the ilikeRadio service** introduced **throughout this January and this February:**

1. Thanks to the work of [@itsdefnotleon](https://x.com/itsdefnotleon)\*<sub>1</sub>, who was now promoted due to his efforts, we're excited to announce a brand new ilikeRadio-related channel to our Discord community. The Now Playing channel uses a Discord webhook to display when ilikeRadio is live, playing songs, and more. We are also able to confirm that we may have the ability to revive the ilikeRadio Discord bot due to his efforts, which we are incredibly thankful for.
2. \*<sub>1</sub> We would also like to thank Typical Nerds from [https://typicalnerds.uk](https://t.co/kNQWko3RNT) for his efforts to support ilikeRadio into transitioning into new systems also, just like [@itsdefnotleon](https://x.com/itsdefnotleon).
3. As we continue to test our brand new systems out for ilikeRadio, thanks to Typical Nerds from [https://typicalnerds.uk](https://t.co/kNQWko4pDr) and [@itsdefnotleon](https://x.com/itsdefnotleon), our main ilikeRadio website ([https://ilikeradio.ismyradio.com](https://t.co/cnZj08qzAV)) will start to broadcast 24 hours a day\*<sub>2</sub> instead of broadcasting when a show airs.
4. \*<sub>2</sub> As we begin 24/7 broadcasts here on ilikeRadio, listeners may hear some new jingles being aired between songs. These are part of a new jingle package that will be fully introduced very soon.

We at ilikeRadio **hope you enjoy this new changes releasing** and we **thank you all for your incredible support towards the ilikeRadio service!**
