## Notice regarding the Schlep situation (24/10/2025)

Today, we **announced a change in how ilikeNews will cover** **Roblox-related topics;** going forward, we're no longer posting updates about **sensitive** or **harmful content**, including **child safety incidents on Roblox**, **lawsuits**, and the currently ongoing **Schlep situation**.

This decision is driven by our commitment to **responsible reporting** and **maintaining a platform that focuses on developments** that are **appropriate for general audiences**. Covering these types of content often involves **distressing** or highly **sensitive matters**, which can be misleading or harmful if presented out of context.

While we will continue to report on **Roblox**, **gaming**, and **technology**, our focus will shift to i**nformative** and **engaging content** that **avoids spreading negativity and potentially distressing stories**, which allows us to **maintain the integrity** of our platform and **prioritise news coverage that is meaningful** without focusing on topics that may be unsafe.

We appreciate your **understanding** as we take this step to refine the focus of our news coverage while continuing to provide **insightful** and **reliable news updates**.

## An ilikeNews Roblox story: 15-year-old missing after Roblox meetup in NYC, family says (26/01/2026)

At ilike Presents, we **express our deepest condolences to the family of 15-year-old Thomas Medlin**, after his family says **he went missing after meeting someone from the Roblox platform in New York City**.

**Authorities have confirmed that Thomas Medlin** was last seen **after travelling to New York City** to **meet an individual he had reportedly interacted with on the platform**. During this period of time, this situation is actively under investigation by authorities.

We want to be clear that **no conclusions should be drawn during this time as the case continues to develop**. **Our thoughts remain with Thomas’s family, friends, and loved ones** during this deeply distressing time.

This incident **serves as a serious reminder of online safety**, particularly for **young people interacting on social platforms**. We strongly **encourage parents or guardians to remain vigilant** and to **engage in open conversations about online interactions and meeting individuals in real life**.

If you believe **you have any information that may help the local authorities, please contact them immediately**.

**We appreciate the understanding and patience** shown by everyone in our community, and we **remain dedicated to acting responsibly and respectfully** when responding to **significant real-world events.**
