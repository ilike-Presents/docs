## Important notice regarding the blacklist of TheDoctor (29/10/2025)

Effective immediately, following a recent comprehensive investigation **on a former staff member**, we have made the decision to **permanently blacklist ***TheDoctor*** across all subsidiaries** of *ilike’s Productions*.

### Context behind this blacklist

The decision to permanently blacklist *TheDoctor* comes after the discovery of a **serious breach of conduct**, specifically involving inappropriate behaviour that is entirely incompatible with the standards we uphold.

During this comprehensive review, we confirmed that:

* *TheDoctor* knowingly anticipated being sent explicit material from a real‑life friend of his.
  * However, upon receiving this explicit adult content that seemed to originate from PornHub, **he proceeded to display it to others (including ***ilike***, the founder of ***ilike’s Productions***, during a call who stated clearly that he didn’t want to see any explicit material),** despite the content’s inappropriate nature.
    * Although the original material was **slightly blurred due to poor laptop camera quality**, the act of knowingly **obtaining and showing explicit content to others** represents a **completely unacceptable lapse in judgement**.
      * **Given the approximate age of ≈13**, **proper parental controls should be put in place** to **prevent access to explicit content** in the first place.
* He also **used racially offensive language in his personal life** and repeated it **in a WhatsApp conversation**, influenced by peer pressure from a racist person **that he considered a “friend”**. As the founder of ilike’s Productions, **I viewed TheDoctor as a capable individual**, making this behaviour **disappointing and unacceptable**, **falling below the standards expected** within our community.

This behaviour **violates basic standards of professionalism and community safety**. Even a partial display of explicit content in **any non‑permitted environment** is a **major breach and cannot be ignored**.

### Our expectations & policies

Our three core values of **Innovation, Ambition and Authenticity** form the foundation for everything we do here at *ilike’s Productions*. Behaviour that contradicts these principles, **especially actions involving explicit content, misconduct, or unsafe behaviour**, has **no place at ** *ilike’s Productions*. We act decisively to protect our community, staff, and audience.

We also hold **an extremely strict zero‑tolerance policy** for behaviour involving **explicit material, exploitation, manipulation, and any other type of conduct** that risks other’s safety.

If an individual knowingly engages or promotes behaviour of this nature, they will be **immediately and permanently blacklisted** from *ilike’s Productions*, regardless of prior involvement.

### Commitment to Transparency

We **understand the importance of communicating clearly and responsibly** in situations such as this. Our decision was made **after reviewing confirmed evidence and ensuring that proper internal procedures were followed**.

We are committed to maintaining an environment built on **professionalism, respect, accountability and the safety for our community members and audience**.

### Closing remarks

After careful consideration, *TheDoctor* will remain **permanently blacklisted from ** *ilike’s Productions*, preventing him to engage in any future **involvement, appearances, contributions or affiliations with us**.

We will continue to **protect our brand, audience, and team** by **upholding the values that define who we are**.
